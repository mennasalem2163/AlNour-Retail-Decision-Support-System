The 9 PM Problem

Egyptian Cabinet Decree 1276 forces all retail shops to close by 9 PM. For Al-Nour Fashion Boutique, this erases the most profitable trading window (21:00–23:00), which historically generated 18–22% of daily revenue. This DSS quantifies the loss and shows managers exactly how to recover it.

Ramadan 2026 additional impact: The closure also eliminates traditional Ramadan night shopping (21:00–01:00), compounding the seasonal revenue hit.

Dashboard Features

Everything a manager needs,
in one place.

7 dashboard pages powered by 5 decision models — from live KPIs to Ramadan-aware seasonal planning.

📊

Operations Overview

KPI cards, pre vs post-regulation hourly revenue comparison, weekly heatmap, and category breakdown — all in one glance.

⚡

Power Hours Optimizer

Composite scoring model ranks every trading hour. Identifies "Power Hours" where deploying max staff and flash deals yields the highest return.

👥

Smart Staffing Planner

Hourly visitor forecasts drive a staffing allocation model. Automatically recommends when to run restocking vs. when every hand must be on the floor.

🔮

What-If Simulator

Adjust closing hour, discount window (%), and marketing spend. The model instantly projects revenue vs. baseline with a waterfall breakdown.

🏆

Competitor Intel

Gap opportunity scores calculated from competitor busy-hour data. Find the exact hours where rivals are quiet and capture uncontested demand.

📈

30-Day Forecast

Exponential smoothing (α=0.25) with linear trend component. Adjustable forecast horizon with ±7% confidence bands.

🌙

Ramadan & Calendar

Full Egyptian retail holiday calendar 2025–2026. Quantifies the Ramadan 2026 revenue loss from the 9 PM closure and shows upcoming seasonal peaks.

💻

CLI Daily Briefing

Run python utils/cli_summary.py for a colour-coded terminal briefing with today's power hours, staffing plan, and top 5 actions.

System Design

Clean MVC Architecture

Model → Controller → View with zero coupling between layers. Every analytical function lives in analytics.py — the view never touches raw data.

DATA LAYER data/pos_transactions.csv 60K+ transactions (2025–2026) data/foot_traffic.csv Hourly visitor counts data/competitor_activity.csv Competitor busy-hour index data/staffing.csv Shift staffing records data/generate_data.py Synthetic generator (CAPMAS/EFG calibrated) MODEL LAYER ← all business logic here models/analytics.py KPIs · Forecast · Power Hours · Staffing · What-If · Gap CONTROLLER LAYER controllers/dashboard_controller.py Routes view ↔ model, formats output VIEW LAYER views/app.py 7-page Streamlit dashboard (Plotly charts) UTILITIES utils/ramadan_calendar.py Egyptian holiday calendar 2025–2026 utils/cli_summary.py Terminal management briefing utils/generate_report.py 13-slide HTML academic report TESTS tests/test_analytics.py 20 pytest unit tests — 100% passing ✅
